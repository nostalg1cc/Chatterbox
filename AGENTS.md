# Nitro

The normal Tauri/React Nitro app is the primary product. `gpui-native/` and
`beta-native/` are experimental tests and out of scope unless the user explicitly
mentions GPUI or the native beta. Do not investigate or modify them by default.

Read **[AGENTS-HANDOFF.md](./AGENTS-HANDOFF.md)** first — it contains the full project context,
stack decisions, Supabase details, and the living checklist of done/todo work.
Keep that checklist updated as you work.
